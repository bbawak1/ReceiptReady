# Project Proposal: ReceiptReady

## Purpose and Value

ReceiptReady is a photo-first expense tracker for small-business owners. Instead of typing every purchase into a spreadsheet, the owner takes a picture of the receipt and the app turns that into an expense record they can categorize, review, and use for tax preparation. The everyday workflow is simple: snap the receipt, confirm the amount and category, and keep going. Over the month, they can see where money is going and export a clean list of business expenses with receipt proof attached.

The value of ReceiptReady comes from how small businesses actually work. Owners are busy, receipts pile up in wallets and camera rolls, and tax time becomes a scramble. A tool that starts with a picture removes that friction: track spending, keep proof, and prepare tax-ready records without a heavy accounting system. For this course, the product is defined around photo capture as the main way to add expenses. Automatic reading of the receipt (OCR) can improve over time; the core system still centers on receipt photos, categories, spending views, and export for tax prep.

## Roles

| Role | Who |
|------|-----|
| **Owner** | Small-business owner who photographs receipts and reviews spending |
| **Admin** | Basic account management (class scope) |

## How it works (photo-first)

1. Take a picture of the receipt  
2. Save it as an expense (with category / tax flag)  
3. See spending totals  
4. Export tax-ready expense report
