# ReceiptReady — Product Backlog

Photo-first small-business expense tracker (take a picture → track spending → tax prep).  
Format matches Sample Product Backlog: **Priority | As a… | I want to… | So that…**  
Lecture 3 form: *As &lt;who&gt;, I want &lt;what&gt;, so that &lt;why&gt;.*

| Priority | As a… | I want to… | So that… |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Owner | take a picture of a receipt | I can capture proof of a purchase quickly |
| 1 | Owner | save a receipt photo as an expense | I can track spending without typing everything from scratch |
| 1 | Owner | view my receipt photos | I can find a purchase I already captured |
| 1 | Owner | choose a category for a receipt expense | I can organize where money is going |
| 1 | Owner | delete a receipt expense | I can remove a capture I do not need |
| 2 | Owner | create an account | I can keep my business receipts saved privately |
| 2 | Owner | log in to my account | I can access my receipt expenses securely |
| 2 | Owner | update my credentials | I can keep my account secure |
| 2 | Owner | delete my account | I can remove my information from the system |
| 2 | Owner | confirm or edit the amount on a receipt expense | I can make sure the saved total is correct |
| 2 | Owner | mark a receipt expense as tax-related | I can prepare business costs for tax time |
| 3 | Owner | view expenses created from receipt photos | I can see my spending history in one place |
| 3 | Owner | filter receipt expenses by category | I can see spending in one area of the business |
| 3 | Owner | filter receipt expenses by date range | I can review a month or quarter of purchases |
| 3 | Owner | view total spending from receipt expenses this month | I can understand current business costs |
| 3 | Owner | view only tax-related receipt expenses | I can focus on records needed for tax preparation |
| 4 | Owner | add a short note to a receipt expense | I can remember the business purpose of the purchase |
| 4 | Owner | retake or replace a receipt photo | I can fix a blurry or incomplete picture |
| 4 | Owner | export receipt expenses to a file | I can share a clean list with a bookkeeper or tax preparer |
| 4 | Owner | export tax-related receipt expenses only | I can create a tax-ready expense report |
| 5 | Owner | have receipt details suggested from the photo | I can spend even less time entering amounts and vendors |
| 5 | Owner | get a reminder to photograph receipts I marked incomplete | I can finish capturing proof before tax time |
| 5 | Admin | deactivate a user account | I can stop access for someone who should no longer use the system |

**Priority guide (like the sample):**
- **1** = take picture → save as expense → categorize
- **2** = account + confirm amount + tax flag
- **3** = spending views / filters
- **4** = notes, retake photo, export for tax
- **5** = later smart-read from photo / reminders

**Build note:** The product is photo-first. Early versions can save the picture and let the owner confirm amount/category; suggesting details from the photo is a later improvement (priority 5).
